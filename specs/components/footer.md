- There shall be a form to contact us

    - Email Address (email input) - Required
    - Message (textarea) - Required

- Then there shall be a list of important links:

  - Home
  - Our Mission
  - Contact us
  - Work with us

- There shall be a list of social media icons (with links to our social media pages):

  - Facebook

- There shall be a list of legal links:

  - Privacy Policy

- There shall be a list of the most important dance styles (with links to their pages):

  - Hip Hop
  - Popping
  - Break dance
  - House
