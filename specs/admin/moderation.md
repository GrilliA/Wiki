# Moderation Page

- There shall be a title at the top: "Moderation"
- There shall be a brief description of the moderation process
- There shall be a tab section with the list of reported/new content (wiki)
  - On click of the reported content,
    there shall be information about the report, the content,
    and the user who reported it
  - There shall be buttons to "Approve" or "Reject" the report
    - Upon clicking "Approve",
      the content shall be removed from the platform,
      and the user who posted it shall receive a notification
    - Upon clicking "Reject",
      the report shall be dismissed,
      and the user who reported it shall receive a notification
- There shall be a tab section with the list of reported users
  - There shall be information about the report and the user
  - Upon clicking "Approve",
    the user shall be banned from the platform,
    and they shall receive an email notification
  - Upon clicking "Reject",
    the report shall be dismissed,
    and the user who reported it shall receive a notification
