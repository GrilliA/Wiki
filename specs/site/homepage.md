# Home page

## Hero Section

- There shall be a welcome message of some sort
- There shall be a search bar, and the CTA shall be the "search" button
  - Once the user starts typing, it shall show suggestions (max 10)
  - Once the enter key is pressed or the search button is clicked, it shall redirect to the search results page

## Top Categories And News

- There shall be a section with the top categories (max 6)
- There shall be a section with the latest news (max 3)

## Our mission

- There shall be a brief description of the mission of the platform
- There shall be info on how to contribute, with a link to the "Work with us" page
- There shall be a "Learn More" button that redirects to the "Our Mission" page
