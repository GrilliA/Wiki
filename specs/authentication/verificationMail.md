# Verification Mail

- The verification email shall contain a link to the verification page.
- The text shall be:

  "Welcome to our platform! Please verify your email address by clicking the link below:

  [Verify Email](#)

  If you did not sign up for this account, please ignore this email.

  Thank you!"

- The verification link shall be valid for 1 month.
- The verification link login the user automatically and redirect to the on boarding form.
