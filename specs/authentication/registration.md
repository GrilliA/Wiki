# Registration

- The Registration shall be in a modal component

- There shall be a registration form with the following fields:

  - Email (email input, required, must be a valid email format)
  - Password (password input, required, must be at least 8 characters long and contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character)
  - Confirm Password (password input, required, must match the Password field)
  - Terms and Conditions (checkbox, required to proceed with registration)

- There shall be a "Register" button to submit the form
- There shall be the possibility to sign up through "google" Button
- There shall be a link to the login page for users who already have an account

- In case of errors (e.g., invalid email format, password too weak, passwords do not match, terms and conditions not accepted), appropriate error messages shall be displayed next to the relevant fields.

- If the registration is successful, the user shall be sent to the verification page (on a new tab)
