# Onboarding

- There shall be a welcome message: "Welcome to our platform!
  We're excited to have you on board.
  Let's get started by setting up your profile."
- Below the welcome message, there shall be a form with the following fields:
  - Profile Picture (file upload) - Optional
  - First Name (text input) - Required (it must be specified that this information won't be public)
  - Last Name (text input) - Required (it must be specified that this information won't be public)
  - Username (text input) - Required, must be unique
  - Bio (textarea with quill editor) - Optional, max 300 characters
- There shall be a "Complete" button to submit the form.
- Once the user clicks "complete", they shall be redirected to the profile.
